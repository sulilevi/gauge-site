Gauge

Privacy Policy

Last updated October 30, 2026

What Gauge collects

Nothing. Gauge has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking, and it never sends data off your device.

What stays on your device

Your timers, their names, recent durations, and the stopwatch with its laps are saved on your device so they survive the app closing. Deleting Gauge deletes them.

Permissions

Gauge asks to schedule alarms so a timer can ring when it ends, and, if you decline that, to send notifications instead. Both are used only for that.
