Timers don't make a sound

Gauge rings timers as alarms, which sound even in silent mode and during a Focus. If you declined alarms when Gauge asked, go to Settings > Gauge and turn on Alarms. Until then, timers notify instead, and notifications are muted in silent mode.

Several timers at once

Tap + to start another timer. The Live Activity shows the one that ends soonest, with a count of the others. Touch and hold a timer to rename it, or swipe it to delete.
